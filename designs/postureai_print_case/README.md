# OffSyn Tech front-layout 3D-print case

The model follows the latest hand sketch and is saved in millimetres.

- Enclosure: 160 W × 240 H × 80 D mm; 3 mm walls.
- Camera opening: Ø23.5 mm; centre 190 mm above the base.
- Ambient-light opening: Ø6 mm; centre 168 mm above the base.
- LCD opening: 95 × 33 mm; centre 135 mm above the base.
- LED opening: Ø8 mm; centre 80 mm above the base.
- Speaker grille opening: 68 × 29 mm; centre 38 mm above the base.

`postureai-front-layout-case.blend` has two objects in the `Printable parts`
collection. The shell and cover export separately as STL. Verify component
dimensions on the physical hardware before printing the final case.
