# Ergo Agent MK-II Production 3D-Print Enclosure

The model follows the production engineering specification and is saved in millimetres.

- Enclosure: 160 W × 240 H × 80 D mm; 3 mm continuous walls with rounded bevels.
- Camera opening: Ø23.5 mm; centre 190 mm above the base with internal support lip.
- Ambient-light opening: Ø6 mm; centre 168 mm above the base.
- LCD opening: 95 × 33 mm; centre 135 mm above the base with snap-fit support.
- LED opening: Ø8 mm; centre 80 mm above the base.
- Speaker grille opening: 68 × 29 mm with 3.4 mm acoustic micro-perforations.
- **USB-C Power Cutout:** 14 mm × 10 mm recessed aperture on left wall (fits standard USB-C strain relief).
- **Thermal Convection Vents:** 7× bottom intake slots and 7× top exhaust louvers for passive thermal management.
- **Internal PCB Standoffs:** 4× M2.5 standoffs spaced at 58 mm × 49 mm matching Raspberry Pi 5 mounting hole pattern.

`postureai-front-layout-case.blend` has two objects in the `Printable parts`
collection. The shell and cover export separately as STL. Printed with PETG/ABS at 0.2mm layer height.
